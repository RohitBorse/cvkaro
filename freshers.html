Questions, answered

Can I upload the resume I already have?

Yes. Upload a PDF or Word file on the ATS checker to score it, or import it into the builder and CVkaro fills in each section for you. Your file is read on your device.

What is an ATS, and why does it matter?

An applicant tracking system is software companies use to collect and filter job applications. It reads your resume, pulls out your details and compares your skills against the job. If it can't read your resume, or your resume doesn't use the job's keywords, a recruiter may never see it.

Is CVkaro really free?

Yes. All templates, downloads and the ATS keyword score are free with no sign-up. Pro AI features are free during our launch period.

Will my resume work on Naukri, LinkedIn and company career sites?

Yes. Downloads are PDFs with real, selectable text, which job portals and applicant tracking systems can read. For the most cautious systems, pick a template marked "ATS-friendly".

Where is my data stored?

In your own browser on this device. If you clear your browser data or switch devices, download your resume first. See our privacy policy for details.

Should my resume be one page?

For students and people with under five years of experience, one page is best. Use the Spacing slider in the Design tab to fit more on a page, and the builder shows a line where a new page starts.

How we score your resume

There's no single official "ATS score". Each company's software works differently. CVkaro's score estimates how well your resume would do when an ATS reads it and a recruiter searches for candidates.

What we checkPointsWhy it matters
Keyword match40Recruiters search ATS databases by skills. Skills marked as required in the job count twice as much as ones listed as "nice to have".
Job title10Recruiters often search by job title first, so your headline or recent titles should use the same words as the job.
Sections and contact15An ATS needs to find your email, phone, experience, education, skills and projects to file your resume correctly.
Measurable impact15Bullet points with numbers ("cut costs by 20%") are more convincing than claims. Full marks when most of your points include a number.
Action verbs10Points that start with verbs like Built, Led or Reduced read as achievements, not duties.
Length10Roughly 200–900 words is ideal for one to two pages.

How we find the job's keywords

We compare the job description with a list of over 200 common skills across tech, data, design, marketing, sales, finance and operations, then add other important words the job repeats. We ignore filler words like "team", "strong" or "opportunity".

We understand common abbreviations

"JS" and "JavaScript", "ML" and "machine learning", or "AWS" and "Amazon Web Services" count as the same skill, so you aren't penalised for writing it differently from the job ad.

Keyword stuffing is penalised

Repeating a keyword many times or listing dozens of skills can raise a match score but looks bad to a real recruiter. We take off up to 5 points when we see it.

What the score can't tell you

No tool can guarantee an interview. The score doesn't judge the quality of your experience, and some companies use AI screening or manual review that works differently. Use the score to find gaps, and only add skills you genuinely have.
